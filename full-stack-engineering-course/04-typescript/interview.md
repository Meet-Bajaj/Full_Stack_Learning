# TypeScript Interview Questions

## Junior
1. What is TypeScript?
2. Explain the difference between `interface` and `type`.
3. What are `any` and `unknown`?

## Mid-Level
1. What are Generics and why do we use them?
2. How does type narrowing work?
3. Explain Discriminated Unions.

## Senior
1. How do Conditional Types work? Provide an example.
2. Explain Covariance and Contravariance in TypeScript.
3. How does the TS Compiler resolve modules?

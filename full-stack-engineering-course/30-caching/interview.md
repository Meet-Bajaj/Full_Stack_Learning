# Caching Interview Framework

## Common Questions
- **Junior:** "What happens when a cache is full?" (Expect: Explanation of eviction policies like LRU).
- **Mid:** "Explain the difference between Cache-Aside and Write-Through. When would you use each?"
- **Senior:** "How would you solve a Cache Stampede in a highly distributed system?" (Expect: Discussion of distributed locking with Redis/Redlock or probabilistic early expiration).

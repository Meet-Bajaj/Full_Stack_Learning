# Lesson 04: Course Structure Guide

This course is vast. To prevent getting lost, every module follows a strict, standardized directory structure. Understanding this structure is key to navigating the curriculum.

## The Module Anatomy

Inside any given module (e.g., `01-javascript-fundamentals`), you will find the following structure:

### 1. `README.md`
The entry point. It contains:
- Learning objectives (what you will know by the end).
- Prerequisites (what you should know before starting).
- High-level overview of the topics covered.

### 2. `lessons/`
This folder contains the core learning material, ordered sequentially (`01-xyz.md`, `02-abc.md`). 
Each lesson includes:
- **Concept Explanation:** The theory.
- **Mental Models & Analogies:** To help you visualize.
- **Code Walkthroughs:** Detailed examples.
- **Best Practices & Pitfalls:** What to do and what to avoid.

### 3. `exercises/exercises.md`
Small, isolated tasks designed to drill the concepts from the lessons. These include:
- Fixing broken code (Debugging).
- Implementing a single function (Coding).
- Tracing execution flow (Reading).

### 4. `projects/projects.md`
Larger, cohesive tasks that combine multiple concepts. Projects escalate in difficulty and resemble real-world tickets you would get on a job.

### 5. `mcqs/mcqs.md`
A massive bank of Multiple Choice Questions. These aren't just "What does `x` do?". They are scenario-based: "Given this architecture, what is the most performant way to...". They include detailed explanations for why the correct answer is right and why the others are wrong.

### 6. `cheatsheet.md`
A highly condensed summary of the module's syntax, commands, and key concepts. Perfect for quick lookups during projects.

### 7. `interview.md`
Common technical and behavioral interview questions related to the module's topics, categorized by Junior, Mid-level, and Senior expectations.

### 8. `assessment.md`
The final boss of the module. A combination of tough MCQs, architecture questions, and a capstone coding challenge. Do not skip this!

### 9. `progress.md`
A checklist. Tick off items as you complete them to track your journey and maintain motivation.

## How to Proceed Through a Module

1. Read the `README.md`.
2. Read Lesson 1.
3. If Lesson 1 has corresponding exercises, do them immediately.
4. Continue through the lessons.
5. Build the Projects.
6. Take the MCQs for active recall.
7. Read the Cheatsheet and Interview questions.
8. Take the Final Assessment.
9. Mark your `progress.md`.

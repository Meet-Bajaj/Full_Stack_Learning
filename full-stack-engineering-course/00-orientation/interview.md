# Module 00 Interview Questions

Since this is an orientation module, these questions focus on soft skills, mindset, and basic tooling knowledge often asked in junior developer interviews.

## Junior Level

**Q: Tell me about a time you were stuck on a programming problem. How did you resolve it?**
**Focus:** The interviewer is looking for your debugging process (reading errors, isolating the issue, Googling effectively) and a growth mindset.

**Q: Why do we use version control systems like Git?**
**Focus:** Discuss tracking history, collaborating with others, and the ability to revert to previous working states.

## Mid-Level

**Q: How do you keep your skills up to date in an industry that changes so rapidly?**
**Focus:** Mention focusing on first principles, reading official documentation over relying solely on tutorials, and understanding trade-offs rather than just jumping on the latest trend.

**Q: Explain the benefit of using Docker in a development environment.**
**Focus:** Address the "works on my machine" problem, environment parity between development and production, and isolation of dependencies (like databases).

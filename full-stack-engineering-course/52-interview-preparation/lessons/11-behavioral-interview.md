# Lesson 11: Behavioral Interviews

## Learning Objectives
- Master the STAR method for answering behavioral questions.
- Understand what companies look for (e.g., Amazon Leadership Principles).
- Prepare stories for common behavioral categories (conflict, failure, leadership).

## 1. The STAR Method

The STAR method is a structured manner of responding to a behavioral-based interview question by discussing the specific **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, and **R**esult.

- **Situation:** Set the scene and give the necessary details of your example. (10%)
- **Task:** Describe what your responsibility was in that situation. (10%)
- **Action:** Explain exactly what steps you took to address it. Use "I" instead of "we" to highlight your specific contributions. (60%)
- **Result:** Share what outcomes your actions achieved. Quantify them if possible. (20%)

## 2. Common Behavioral Categories

### Conflict Resolution
*Question:* Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker or manager.
*What they want:* Empathy, ability to compromise, focusing on data rather than emotion, prioritizing the team's success.

### Failure / Overcoming Obstacles
*Question:* Tell me about a time a project failed or you made a mistake.
*What they want:* Ownership, accountability, learning from mistakes, lack of defensiveness.

### Leadership / Initiative
*Question:* Tell me about a time you went above and beyond your role.
*What they want:* Proactiveness, identifying problems before they are assigned, driving projects to completion.

## 3. The "Tell Me About Yourself" Pitch

Your pitch should be 2-3 minutes max:
1. **Present:** Your current role and main responsibilities.
2. **Past:** A quick highlight reel of how you got here (relevant achievements).
3. **Future:** Why you are excited about this specific opportunity.

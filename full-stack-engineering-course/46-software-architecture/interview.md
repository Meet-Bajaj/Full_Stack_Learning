# Software Architecture Interview Questions

## Junior Level
1. What is the difference between monolithic and microservices architectures?
2. What are non-functional requirements? Give examples.
3. Explain what an API Gateway is and why you might use it.

## Mid Level
1. Describe the core principles of Clean Architecture. How does the Dependency Rule work?
2. When would you choose a Modular Monolith over Microservices?
3. What is the CAP theorem and how does it affect database choice in distributed systems?
4. Explain Event Sourcing and CQRS. What problems do they solve?

## Senior Level
1. Design a scalable rate-limiting system for a public API. Walk through your architectural choices and trade-offs.
2. How do you handle distributed transactions across multiple microservices? Explain the Saga pattern.
3. You inherited a Big Ball of Mud legacy monolith. Outline your architectural strategy to modernize and migrate it to a modular architecture with minimal downtime.
4. How do you approach domain boundaries when defining bounded contexts in DDD?

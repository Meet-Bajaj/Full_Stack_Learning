## 💡 Study Tips

1. **Do not skip modules** — Each builds on the previous
2. **Complete exercises** — Reading is not enough
3. **Build projects** — Apply what you learn
4. **Take MCQs seriously** — They reveal gaps in understanding
5. **Review regularly** — Use spaced repetition
6. **Track progress** — Update your progress.md files
7. **Take breaks** — Avoid burnout with sustainable pace
